Procurement Progress Report — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Procurement Progress Report (“the app”, at hani-procurement.github.io) is a web app that helps a project team track procurement items (PQD and Material Submittal status) in a Google Sheet they own. This page explains what data the app accesses and why.

How the app works

The app runs entirely in your web browser. There is no server or database operated by the developer: when you sign in with Google, your browser talks directly to Google’s APIs using your own Google account. The developer never receives, stores or sees your data.

Google data the app accesses, and why

  • Your email address (userinfo.email) — to identify you and check whether you are a member of a project and what you are allowed to do in it.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets) — to read and update the project spreadsheet you open or create through the app (items, statuses, team members, change history and report settings). The app only works with project spreadsheets you choose to open.
  • Google Drive files created or opened with the app (drive.file) — to create a new project spreadsheet, export it for backups and reports, and share it with the team members an Admin adds. The app cannot see other files in your Drive.
  • Send email (gmail.send) — used only when a project Admin sends a backup or a status report (by clicking “Send now” or by turning on a schedule). The email is sent from that Admin’s own Gmail to the address the Admin entered. The app cannot read your mailbox.

Where data is stored

All project data stays in the Google Sheet in the project owner’s Google Drive. Your browser may keep small local items (for example your last opened project, display preferences, and changes made while offline that are waiting to be sent); these stay on your device and can be cleared from your browser settings.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data is only visible to the people the project Admin has added to that project, through Google Sheets’ own sharing. The app does not use your data for advertising or to train any AI model.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app’s access to your Google account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. A project Admin can also remove you from a project in the app’s Control Panel, which also removes your access to its spreadsheet.
